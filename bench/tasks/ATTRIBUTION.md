`gsm8k-sample.jsonl` holds 40 problems (every 33rd, from index 0) of the GSM8K test split,
from https://github.com/openai/grade-school-math (MIT licence, Copyright (c) 2021 OpenAI).
Only the question and the final numeric answer are kept. All other tasks are generated by
`bench/tasks.ts` from a fixed seed.
